## 1. Quick start / شروع کیسے کریں

**Roman Urdu:** Counter kholne ke baad pehle zaban chunein (Settings mein kabhi bhi badal sakte hain). Agar restaurant pehli dafa bana rahe hain to **Register** chunein. Pehle se account hai to **Login** chunein.

**English:** Open the counter, pick a language, then either register a new restaurant or log in to an existing one.

---

## 2. New restaurant registration / نیا ریسٹورنٹ رجسٹر کرنا

**Roman Urdu:**
1. Register chunein.
2. Restaurant ka naam, PIN (4 se 6 hindse), phone number aur city likhein.
3. Plan chunein: **Free trial** (14 din) ya **Paid plan** (bank transfer ke baad admin confirm karega).
4. "Register and start" dabayein.
5. Aapko **recovery code** dikhega (RC-XXXX…). Ye sirf ek dafa dikhta hai. Isay kisi mehfooz jagah likh lein ya copy kar lein.

**Important:**
- Ek phone number par ek hi restaurant register ho sakta hai.
- Naam bilkul ya bahut milta-julta naam dobara register nahi ho sakta.
- Ek device par zyada se zyada 2 restaurants register ho sakte hain.

**English:** Enter name, 4–6 digit PIN, phone and plan. You get a recovery code once. Save it. One phone number can register only one restaurant, and very similar names are blocked to prevent repeated free trials.

---

## 3. Login and recovery code / لاگ ان اور ریکوری کوڈ

**Roman Urdu:**
- Apne registered device par: naam + PIN daal kar login karein. Recovery code ki zaroorat nahi.
- Naye device par: naam + PIN + **recovery code** daalein.
- Agar aap ne browser data clear kar diya ya phone badal diya, to recovery code se login hoga.
- Shift khatam karne ke liye Settings → Shift → **Close Shift / EOD report** chunein.

**English:** Same device logs in with name and PIN. A new device needs the recovery code too. Close the shift from Settings → Shift.

**Note:** Agar restaurant kisi aur device ya tab par active hai, to pehle wahan shift band karein ya 45 second baad dobara try karein.

## 4. Counter dashboard / کاؤنٹر ڈیش بورڈ

**Roman Urdu:** Sidebar (desktop) ya neeche ki bar (mobile) se sections badle:
- **Orders** — live orders, timer aur bill.
- **Menu Editor** — items, price, stock, translations.
- **Costing** — recipe aur margin.
- **QR Center** — table QR codes.
- **Subscription** — plan aur payment.
- **Settings** — sab ترتیبات.

Upar ke cards mein: active tickets, naye orders aur shift ki sales dikhti hain.

**English:** Use the sidebar (desktop) or bottom bar (mobile) to switch sections. The top cards show active tickets, new orders and shift sales.

---

## 5. Orders, timer and billing / آرڈر، ٹائمر اور بلنگ

**Roman Urdu — order ka flow:**
1. Naya order aata hai (New). Ek chhota alert aur awaaz aati hai.
2. **Accept & cook** dabayein. Chahen to pehle "Min" box mein tayari ke minutes likhein — customer ko live countdown dikhega ("Ready in mm:ss", agar deri ho to "Late by").
3. Khana tayar hone par **Mark served** dabayein.
4. Served ke baad "Billing pending" dikhta hai. Customer ko "Please pay at counter" dikhta hai.
5. Payment mil jaye to **Mark paid / closed** dabayein. Order active list se hat jata hai.

**Printing:** Har order par **KOT** (kitchen ticket) aur **Bill** ke buttons hain. Bill par NTN / STRN number bhi print hota hai (Settings → NTN / STRN mein set karein).

**Alerts:** Customer agar **Water**, **Waiter** ya **Bill** ki request bheje to card par red/blue tag aata hai. "Clear flag" se hata dein.

**English:** New → Accept & cook (optional prep minutes) → Mark served → Mark paid / closed. KOT and Bill can be printed. Customer requests show as tags, clear them with "Clear flag".

## 6. Menu editor, stock and translations / مینو، اسٹاک اور ترجمہ

**Roman Urdu:**
- **Naya item:** Category, naam, price likhein aur "+ Add item" dabayein.
- **Photo:** Item ke box par camera ka icon dabayein aur tasveer chunein.
- **Badge:** jaise "Chef Special". Customer ko naam ke saath dikhega.
- **Stock:** khali chhodein to stock unlimited hai. Number likhein to wo itne plate tak chalega. **0** likhne se item "Sold out" ho jata hai. Order aate hi stock kam hota hai, aur order cancel ho to wapas barh jata hai.
- **Available / Sold out:** button se item ko foran band ya chalu karein.
- **Translate (naya):** har item ke saath **Translate** button hai. Language chunein, us zaban mein item ka naam likhein aur bahar click karein. Customer aur counter us zaban mein naam dekhenge. Jo zaban select nahi hogi wahan original naam dikhega.
- **Delete:** 🗑️ se item hatayein (confirm hoga).

**English:** Add items with category, name and price. Stock blank = unlimited, 0 = sold out (it reduces automatically per order and is restored on cancel). **Translate** lets you add the item name in any supported language; customers see it in their chosen language.

**Note:** Translation sirf item ke naam ke liye hai. Item ki description aur category ka naam abhi translate nahi hota. (Coming Soon)

## 7. Deals and promo popup / ڈیلز اور پروموشن

**Roman Urdu:**
- **Deals / Combos** tab mein combo banayein (naam, items ki tafseel, price, purani price, dates, mood). Deal ko Active / Off kar sakte hain.
- **Promo** (Settings → Promo): ek text ya tasveer likh kar customer ke saamne popup dikhayein. Customer ek dafa "OK" dabaye to dobara nahi dikhega jab tak promo badle.

**English:** Deals are combo offers with optional dates and mood tags. Promo shows a popup to customers once per promo version.

## 8. QR codes and tables / QR کوڈ اور ٹیبل

**Roman Urdu:** QR Center se har table ka QR download karein aur table par lagayein. Customer QR scan kare to menu khulta hai. Table band hone ya QR badalne ki zaroorat ho to QR Center se naya QR banayein.

**English:** Generate and print table QR codes from QR Center. Customers scan to open the menu for their table.

---

## 9. Delivery areas and riders / ڈیلیوری اور رائیڈرز

**Roman Urdu:**
- Settings → Delivery mein areas aur charge add karein.
- Rider ke liye counter se permanent Rider App link banta hai. Rider usi link se login karke delivery ka status update karta hai aur apni location bhejta hai.
- Customer ke orders page par rider ka naam, rating aur location dikhti hai. Delivery complete rider portal se hoti hai.

**English:** Add delivery areas in Settings. Riders use a permanent link from the counter to update delivery status and location.

## 11. Subscription and renewal / سبسکرپشن اور تجدید

**Roman Urdu:**
- Subscription tab mein plan, baqi din aur payment accounts dikhte hain.
- Paid plan ke liye admin ke bataye gaye bank/JazzCash account par rakam bhejein aur receipt/reference dein. Admin confirm karega, phir plan chalu ho jayega.
- Muddat khatam ho to grace period milta hai. Us ke baad account "sold out" ho jata hai, lekin data safe rehta hai.

**English:** Pay the shown account and submit the payment. Admin confirms. After expiry there is a grace period, then the account is marked sold out with data kept safe.

## 12. Customer guide / کسٹمر کے لیے گائیڈ

**Roman Urdu:**
1. QR scan karein. Pehle zaban chunein (header ke button se badal sakte hain).
2. Menu se item par **+** dabayein. Mood chunein ya note likhein.
3. **View Cart** kholen. Counter ya Delivery chunein. Delivery ke liye naam, phone, pata aur area dein.
4. Payment ka tareeqa chunein. Online ho to QR scan karke rakam bhejein.
5. **Place order** dabayein. Orders page par status dikhta hai: انتظار، تیار ہو رہا ہے، پیش کیا گیا۔
6. Madad chahiye to neeche ke buttons se paani, waiter ya bill ki request bhejein.

**English:** Scan QR, choose language, add items, open the cart, pick Counter or Delivery, choose payment, place the order and follow its status. Use the floating buttons for water, waiter or bill.

## 13. Language support / زبانیں

**Roman Urdu:**
- Ye app 25 zabanon mein chalaya ja sakta hai (zaban ka option Settings aur customer header mein hai).
- **Urdu aur Arabic** mein poora interface (counter, customer, settings, menu) translate hai. Arabic chunne par page RTL (dayein se bayein) ho jata hai.
- Baqi zabanon mein jo text abhi translate nahi hua wo English mein dikhega. Aisa hone par koi screen kharab nahi hoti.
- Menu ke naam har zaban mein Menu Editor ke **Translate** se add kar sakte hain.

**English:** Urdu and Arabic are fully translated (RTL for both). Other languages fall back to English for missing text. Menu item names can be translated per language from the Menu Editor.

## 14. Troubleshooting / مسائل کا حل

| Masla | Hal |
|---|---|
| "Ye restaurant abhi kisi doosri device par active hai" | Doosri device par Shift Close karein, ya 45 second baad try karein. |
| Naye device par login nahi ho raha | Recovery code daalein (Register ke waqt diya gaya). |
| "Restaurant Name ya PIN ghalat hai" | Naam aur PIN dobara check karein. PIN bhool gaye to support se rabta karein. |
| Customer ko menu nahi dikh raha | Menu Editor mein items check karein. Item "Available" ho aur stock 0 na ho. |
| Customer ko naya item nahi dikh raha | Page refresh karein ya QR dobara scan karein. |
| Bill par NTN/STRN nahi aa raha | Settings → NTN / STRN mein number save karein. Ye sirf customer bill par print hota hai. |
| Language badalne par kuch English hai | Wo text abhi translate nahi hua. Urdu/Arabic mein poora page milega. |
