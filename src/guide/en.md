# Table Order — Guide book

This guide explains each part of the app. Open **Settings** and pick a section from the top bar to change that part of your restaurant.

## Language

Choose the app language under **Language**. The counter and the customer menu switch at once on this device. Arabic, Urdu, Persian and Pashto show the page right-to-left.

## Restaurant

Set the restaurant name, description, address, contact phone, business hours and the currency label.

## Branding

Upload your logo under **Logo & theme**. Colours and the display font are matched automatically from the logo, for the counter and the customer menu.

## Branches

Manage your branch locations. The customer-facing name stays the same. To add a new physical location, send a request; the admin approves it. You do not need a new activation code.

## Payment

Turn on cash payment and/or online payment. Online payment is a manual method (for example JazzCash, Easypaisa or a bank QR): you enter the method name, account title and instructions, and upload a payment QR image. Customers scan the QR and transfer the money themselves. This is not a payment gateway.

## Subscription

See your subscription and renewal details.

## Security

Change the restaurant login PIN. Enter the current PIN, then the new PIN (4 to 6 digits), and confirm it. The PIN is stored in hashed form.

## Tax

Set up the tax settings for your restaurant. Check the values with your accountant before you change them.

## Delivery

Manage delivery areas here. Riders do not need a QR code. A permanent Rider App link is created from the counter.

## Alerts

Choose the sound and vibration for new orders, and turn order, water request and waiter call alerts on or off. These preferences are saved on this device.

## Printing

Set receipt options: show tax, show logo (where supported) and the footer message.

## WhatsApp

Optional. Save a WhatsApp number and a WhatsApp button appears on the customer menu.

## Shift

Close the shift and print the end-of-day (EOD) report, or log out of this device.

## Promo

Create and manage promotions for your customers.

## NTN / STRN

Enter your NTN and STRN tax registration numbers for the bill.

## Guide book

This page. It always shows the guide in the language you have chosen for the app.
